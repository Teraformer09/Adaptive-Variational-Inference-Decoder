# End-to-End Implementation Policy, Curriculum Training, and Ablation Study

## 1. Six-Phase Modular Implementation Policy

The AVID World Model (`world_model/`) is structured into six modular phases that enforce a strict boundary between observable hardware telemetry and simulator-only ground truth. In **Phase 1 (`world_model/data/`)**, the trajectory generator (`generator.py`) synthesizes multi-round trajectories across the 17-node Surface-17 bipartite processor graph for all five isolated noise families (`B1`, `A1`, `C1`, `C2`, `A2`) and the combined mixed-noise regime (`MIXED_ALL_THREE`). Every generated record is validated against `world_model/dataset/trajectory.schema.json` via `schema_validator.py`, verified for zero seed or trajectory-ID overlap across splits (`train`, `val`, `test_id`, `test_ood_schedule`), and sliced by `replay_dataset.py` into overlapping R2D2 replay windows of length `burn_in_steps + train_steps + horizon_max`.

In **Phase 2 (`world_model/perception/`)**, `feature_stems.py` and `graph_encoder.py` implement the non-vision `QuantumGraphPerceptionEncoder`. Rather than applying image convolutions, the perception layer embeds binary detection events, raw stabilizer parities, validity-masked continuous analog readout log-likelihood ratios and calibration telemetry, two-dimensional Gaussian radial basis function (RBF) inter-qubit distances, and schedule-conditioned coupler edges before performing multi-hop message passing across the processor graph.

In **Phase 3 (`world_model/models/`)**, `latent_space.py` defines the five factored physical latent distributions (`z_corr`, `z_leak`, `z_drift`, `z_memory`, `z_event`), while `r2d2_rssm_core.py` and `st_transformer_core.py` implement the two interchangeable temporal backbones (**Version A: `R2D2-Graph-RSSM`** with Chrono-Initialized dual-timescale LSTM cells and R2D2 burn-in warmup; **Version B: `Small Spatio-Temporal Causal Transformer`**). Both backbones are wrapped by `AVIDWorldModel` (`world_model.py`), which enforces the inference contract by raising a `ValueError` if `latent_truth` is passed to `.predict()`.

In **Phase 4 (`world_model/heads/`)**, `prediction_heads.py` decodes the six physics-specific output heads (Detection, Correlation, Leakage, Drift/Memory, Calibration, and Common-Event), and `elbo_loss.py` computes the multi-task sequential variational ELBO objective across forecast horizons $H \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$. Finally, **Phase 5 (`world_model/train.py`)** executes the three-stage training curriculum and architectural ablation suite, and **Phase 6 (`world_model/evaluate_vs_baselines.py`)** benchmarks all trained models against the QuTiP classical baselines (`B0–B5`) on held-out in-distribution and out-of-distribution schedule trajectories.

## 2. Three-Stage Training Curriculum and Seven-Variant Ablation Matrix

Training proceeds through a three-stage curriculum designed to prevent posterior collapse and cross-mechanism conflation when multiple noise sources act simultaneously. **Stage I (Single-Family Warmup)** trains the model on isolated trajectories from each individual noise family (`B1`, `A1`, `C1`, `C2`, `A2`) over short forecast horizons ($H \in \{1, 2\}$) with linear $\beta$-KL warmup, anchoring each of the five latent subspaces to its corresponding physical mechanism. **Stage II (Mixed-Noise Disentanglement)** transitions to joint training across `MIXED_ALL_THREE` trajectories over the full forecast horizon set ($H \in \{1, 2, 4, 8\}$), forcing the correlation, leakage, burst, and drift/memory heads to disentangle coexisting faults. **Stage III (Inference-Contract Verification)** validates that `.predict()` operates strictly on `strip_latent_truth()` sanitized observations and rejects any call containing simulator ground truth.

To prove the necessity of each architectural inductive bias, the pipeline trains and evaluates seven controlled variants under identical optimization budgets:
1. **`WM_R2D2_Graph_RSSM_Full`**: Full Version A backbone combining `GATv2` graph perception, Chrono-Initialized dual-timescale LSTM-RSSM memory, R2D2 burn-in warmup (`burn_in_steps = 16`), schedule conditioning, and analog/telemetry conditioning.
2. **`WM_Small_ST_Transformer_Full`**: Full Version B backbone combining edge-biased graph self-attention with causal temporal self-attention and context warmup (`burn_in_steps = 16`).
3. **`Ablation_No_R2D2_BurnIn` (`burn_in_steps = 0`)**: Initializes recurrent memory from zeros at the start of every evaluation window, measuring cold-start degradation under partial observability on multi-round leakage (`B1`) and TLS drift (`C1/C2`).
4. **`Ablation_No_Spatial_Graph` (`spatial_mode = "none"`)**: Replaces graph message passing with node-local MLPs, demonstrating that spatial coupling is required to resolve parallel gate crosstalk (`A1`) and radial phonon bursts (`A2`).
5. **`Ablation_No_Temporal_Memory` (`temporal_mode = "none"`)**: Removes recurrent and causal temporal memory, demonstrating that sequential state tracking is required to capture multi-round leakage persistence (`B1`) and non-Markovian memory revivals (`C1/C2`).
6. **`Ablation_No_Schedule_Cond` (`use_schedule = False`)**: Masks the gate schedule $u_t$, demonstrating that schedule conditioning is essential for predicting correlated crosstalk (`A1`), particularly on unseen three-gate concurrent schedules (`OOD`).
7. **`Ablation_Syndrome_Only` (`use_analog = False`)**: Masks analog readout LLRs and calibration telemetry, quantifying the observational identifiability gap between binary syndromes and soft analog readout channels.

## 3. Low-Rank Regime Adapters and Hugging Face Hub Export (`world_model/hf_export.py`)

To support rapid specialization to individual hardware noise regimes or newly calibrated gate schedules without fine-tuning the full world model, `world_model/models/adapters.py` implements modular Low-Rank Adaptation (`LoRA`) and residual calibration modules (`NoiseRegimeAdapter`). Once the main world model (`WM_R2D2_Graph_RSSM_Full`) is trained, its base parameters are frozen and five compact adapters ($\approx 3\text{K}$ parameters each) are trained for specialized deployment: `adapter_B1_transmon_leakage`, `adapter_A1_gate_crosstalk`, `adapter_C1_C2_tls_idle_memory`, `adapter_A2_chipwide_events`, and `adapter_OOD_schedule_calibration`.

The `world_model/hf_export.py` pipeline packages the two main pretrained world models (`main_models/`), all five LoRA regime adapters (`adapters/`), the seven ablation checkpoints (`checkpoints/`), the evaluation metrics and 300-DPI diagnostic figures (`evaluation/`), `config.json`, and an auto-generated Model Card (`README.md`) into `world_model/hf_hub_bundle/` and uploads the complete artifact bundle directly to Hugging Face Hub via `huggingface_hub`.
