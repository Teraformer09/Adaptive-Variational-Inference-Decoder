## 3. Verified Architectural Discrepancies & High-Risk Failure Modes (`DEF-L4-01` .. `DEF-L4-12`)

### 3.1 Assessment of the Three Highest-Risk Failure Modes
1. **Risk 1 — False Transaction Completion vs Acceptance Conflation (`DEF-L4-05`):**
   - `cosim_bridge_main.cpp:248-262` executes `rtl.decode_syndrome(...)` **synchronously to completion** (`22` cycles at `K=3`, `24` cycles in OSD Mode 2) before returning a single JSON response containing `"accepted_cycle": tr.end_cycle`.
   - **Consequence:** On the positive side, the bridge never claims completion before `m_axis_correction_tvalid` fires; **however**, `"accepted_cycle"` actually reports **`C_completed` (`tr.end_cycle`), NOT `C_accepted` (`start_cycle` / `s_axis_tvalid && s_axis_tready`)**, `completed_cycle` is completely absent from the JSON RPC response schema, and the synchronous blocking loop makes it **architecturally impossible over the Unix socket** to issue a concurrent `MMIO_WRITE` (`COMMIT`) or `RESET` while `bp_busy == 1`!
2. **Risk 2 — Epoch & Register-Map Disagreement (`DEF-L4-01`, `DEF-L4-02`, `DEF-L4-03`):**
   - `architecture/contracts/rtl_software_interface.yaml` swaps `0x04C` (`STATUS`) and `0x054` (`cfg_max_iter`).
   - `architecture/contracts/weight_commit_protocol.yaml` swaps `status_reg[0]` (`swap_pending`) and `status_reg[1]` (`bp_busy`).
   - `weight_reg_driver.{h,c}` + `physical_backend.cpp` (`AVID_HAL_BACKEND_PHYSICAL`) treat `0x024..0x044` as read-only active weights instead of `shadow_z[0..8]`, discard `llr_z`, read `epoch` from `0x04C[31:16]` (hardcoded `0` in RTL!), and run a non-equivalent BP engine (`*corr_x = 0`).
   - Meanwhile, `simulation_backend.cpp:349-354` parses `corr_x`, `corr_z`, `bp_cycles`, `axis_total_cycles` from `SYNDROME_TRANSFER` responses **but discards `epoch_irq`**!
3. **Risk 3 — Timing & Fidelity Distortion (`DEF-L4-08`, `DEF-L4-09`):**
   - Neither `verilator` nor `qemu-arm`/`qemu-aarch64` is installed in the baseline host environment; `emulator/bridge/CMakeLists.txt` compiles `cpp_cycle_exact_reference_fallback` unconditionally, and `emulator/qemu/run.sh` silently `exec`s native `avid_daemon_x86_64` when QEMU is absent!
   - Even between `USE_VERILATOR=1` and `#else` fallback inside `verilator_rtl_driver.h`, `reset_model(rcyc)` advances `rcyc + 2` cycles in Verilator (`24` cycles after double startup reset) vs `rcyc` (`20` cycles) in fallback, and `decode_syndrome()` splits `2` ingress + `3` egress cycles in Verilator vs `3` + `2` in fallback.
